Who are these people?

Balkan ABBA?

Melbourne's enchanting contemporary world music group, The Royal High Jinx, has taken the Australian festival scene by storm, infusing stages with originality, virtuosity and theatrical flair. Their music draws from European traditions, adorned with gypsy jazz, Latin, Balkan & swing rhythms, and a touch of folk cabaret circus.

They've captivated European audiences on their "Gone Gone Gone" Germany tour and have played hundreds of shows in Australia and internationally. With three dynamic albums under their belt, they've been described as the "Balkan ABBA" for their catchy songs, infectious tunes and glamorous show.

As nominees for the AFMA People's Choice Award in 2023 and 2024, they are thrilled to celebrate with you, whether you're a fun-loving festival-goer or a fan of intimate house concerts. Immerse yourself in a world of excitement as Melbourne's most vibrant band joins you for a fun-filled night of singing, dancing and happiness.

For festivals & events

Dance workshops

In this interactive, practical session, Terry leads you step by step through dances from around the world, while Jen, Sarah and Silas provide a spicy live soundtrack of traditional world music songs and tunes that will bring rhythm to even the guiltiest of feet. (Yes, that's a George Michael reference.)

Beginner or seasoned dancer, Terry's unique and hilarious teaching style guarantees a great time while you learn several fun and unusual dances from all over the world. Bring your dancing shoes to a Royal High Jinx Dance Party!
